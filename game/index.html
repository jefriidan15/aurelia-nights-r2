AURELIA NIGHTS

Port Aurelia. One driver. Six jobs. No way out but through.

Rook Vane owes the harbour syndicate fifty grand. The only way to pay it back is to run their jobs — steal, dodge the Port Aurelia PD, and stay alive until the last checkpoint falls.

Aurelia Nights · an original open-world crime game · keyboard + mouse · sound on for the full cut

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / arrowsMove on foot / drive
Mouse + clickAim and shoot (when armed)
EEnter / exit a car
FSteal a parked car (raises wanted)
QSwitch weapon
RReload
P / EscPause
MMute sound

Follow the yellow marker. Finish all six missions to win. Getting wasted sends you to St. Marrow Hospital ($200). Getting busted by the PD fails the mission. Spray shops repaint your car and clear your heat — for a price. The diner heals you. The safehouse saves your game and cools your wanted level.
