HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD walk, Shift sprint, E enter a car, Mouse aim, Left click fire. Pistol first, shotgun later.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A D steer, Space handbrake, E exit the car, H horn.

Crime: stealing a car, shooting, or ramming police raises your wanted level (1–5 stars). Cops hunt you harder with every star. Lay low to cool off, or pay $100 at a Spray Shop to wipe it clean and repair your car.

Shops: stand by a shop door and press E — Spray Shop clears wanted, Gun Shop sells guns & ammo, Diner heals, Hospital fully heals, Safehouse saves the game. Green $ pickups on the street are free cash.

Missions: press M, pick a job, follow the yellow marker. Finish all six to own the night.
