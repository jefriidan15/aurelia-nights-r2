AURELIA NIGHTS

Port Aurelia never sleeps. Neither do you.

You are Aurelia, driver-for-hire in Rosa's crew. Six jobs stand between you and the crown of Port Aurelia — steal, survive, shoot, and outrun the whole Port Aurelia PD. The harbour is yours if you can take it.

A top-down open-world crime game. All art, story and sound are original.

HOW TO PLAY

On foot

WASD walk · Shift sprint · E steal / enter car
Mouse aim · Click fire · 1 pistol / 2 shotgun

Driving

W accelerate · S brake / reverse · A D steer
Space handbrake · E exit car · H horn

Heat

Stealing, shooting and ramming cops raise your stars. Lay low to cool off, or pay $100 at Lola's Spray Shop.

Keys

M job board · P pause · F1 mute sound

Shops

Spray, guns, diner, hospital, safehouse (save).
Grab street cash — $25 to $75 a pickup.

ROSA'S JOB BOARD

Finish all six jobs to become King of Port Aurelia.

PAUSED

Port Aurelia waits for no one. Except now.

BUSTED

The PD caught up with you. Fine paid, heat cleared.

WASTED

St. Aurelia General patched you up. It cost you.

KING OF PORT AURELIA

All six jobs complete. The harbour is yours.

THE END
